# NNNN - Title stating the decision

## Context

The situation that forces a choice. What constraints apply, what was known at the time. Written so it still makes sense to someone who was not there.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Alternatives considered

Each option that was genuinely on the table, and the specific reason it was not chosen. An ADR with no alternatives is a note, not a decision record.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it commits the project to. Include the costs, not only the benefits.
